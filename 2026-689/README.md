# Access request 2026-689 — Correspondence between the borough and the English Montreal School Board (EMSB) on rue de Terrebonne since January 1, 2023

**Public body:** Arrondissement de Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, Division du greffe
**Filed:** October 29, 2026 (the response gives that date of receipt)
**Answered:** November 26, 2026, 9:22, by the secrétaire d'arrondissement
**Released:** one PDF of 32 pages (e-mail threads of June 2024 to February 2025 printed from the access office's mailbox on November 19, 2026, the EMSB's letter of November 22, 2024 and the e-mail that sent it to the council members), with the notice of recourse and the text of ss. 53, 54, 57 and 59; personal information of private persons blacked out

## What was asked

The request ([raw/request_2026-10-29_form_text_fr.txt](raw/request_2026-10-29_form_text_fr.txt), the text entered in the borough's form) asked for four things: (1) the EMSB's letter of November 22, 2024 on the Terrebonne bike path, tabled as correspondence at the borough council of December 2, 2024, with its attachments and the e-mail that transmitted it; (2) any reply by the borough to that letter and any acknowledgement; (3) any other correspondence exchanged with the EMSB since January 1, 2023 about rue de Terrebonne, its bike path or safety around the Willingdon and St. Monica schools; (4) any borough document on the school-bus incident mentioned in the letter.

## What the response says

The response ([raw/response_2026-11-26_message_text_fr.txt](raw/response_2026-11-26_message_text_fr.txt), the text of the covering e-mail; the requester's e-mail address removed) says the borough encloses « la correspondance échangée entre l'EMSB et l'arrondissement, la lettre de la Commission scolaire English-Montréal (EMSB) datée du 22 novembre 2024 ainsi que le courriel avisant les membres du cabinet du dépôt de la lettre au Conseil d'arrondissement du 2 décembre 2024 », and that « certaines informations personnelles » were omitted under ss. 53, 54, 57 and 59 of the Act ([raw/Articles_53_54_57_59.pdf](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Articles_53_54_57_59.pdf); notice of recourse: [raw/Avis_de_recours.pdf](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Avis_de_recours.pdf)). It does not answer the four items one by one: it does not say whether a reply to the letter of November 22, 2024 (item 2) or a document on the school-bus incident (item 4) exists.

### The parents' drop-off at the Mackay Centre and Philip E. Layton schools, June 2024 ([pp. 1–5](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=1))

On June 26, 2024 the division wrote to the principal that, after exchanges with the ombudsman's office about parents' concerns, « nous pensons qu'il serait souhaitable que l'école accommode les parents avec un débarcadère sur le terrain de l'école »; that « même si vous êtes en mesure d'accommoder les parents sur votre terrain, nous allons maintenir un débarcadère sur le domaine public. Actuellement, celui-ci est prévu sur le côté sud de la rue Terrebonne, immédiatement à l'est de la rue Benny »; and proposed instead the east side of rue Benny, « une rue locale apaisée, avec moins de 1000 véhicules par jour (comparativement à 3000-4000 sur Terrebonne) et sans aménagement cyclable » ([pp. 3–4](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=3)). The principal answered in the body of that message that the school's grounds could not take parents' cars (« Il n'y a tout simplement pas de places de stationnement pour nos parents au début et à la fin des cours »; « la trentaine de véhicules (autobus et minivans) transportant nos élèves ») and that « Le débarcadère doit absolûment demeurer à son emplacement actuel pour assurer la sécurité de tous à long terme » ([p. 4](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=4)). After a visit by the division on June 27, 2024, the principal wrote: « la meilleure solution pour notre communauté à mobilité réduite (non en chaise roulante) serait de conserver le débarcadère à l'endroit actuel. Les parents dont les enfants son[t] en chaise roulante utilisent les stationnements pour handicappés sur le terrain de l'école et non le débarcadère. » ([p. 1](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=1)).

### After the lanes were built, August to November 2024 ([pp. 9–12](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=9))

On August 15, 2024 the division wrote to the principals of four schools that the lanes and the one-way had been put in place in July, that « mis à part un débarcadère en face de l'école MAB-Mackay qui a été déplacé sur la rue Benny, tous les débarcadères scolaires sont demeurés identiques à proximité des écoles », that it would monitor on the ground and had asked the police for increased surveillance near the schools, and: « Nous sommes aussi disponibles à vous écoutez et à bonifier/ajuster l'aménagement afin d'optimiser la sécurité de tous les usagers. » ([p. 11](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=11)). The Mackay / Layton principal asked whether the green posts would be removed for the winter « ou est-ce que la ville considère que la piste cyclable est ouverte à l'année? » ([p. 11](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=11); sent November 4, 2024 per [p. 10](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=10)). The division answered the same day: « L'arrondissement CDN-NDG a annoncé officiellement les aménagements cyclables qui seront maintenus et déneigés l'hiver prochain et Terrebonne en fait partie »; « nous allons enlever environ 50% des bollards afin de faciliter les opérations de déneigement »; « J'ai aussi fait des observations en personne à la rentrée du matin et à la sortie du soir tout semble plutôt bien se dérouler »; the no-stopping zone at Terrebonne and Benny had been enlarged on October 17 for the school's buses; « Est-ce que des enjeux demeurent? » ([p. 10](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=10)). The principal replied (header date November 5, 2024, [p. 9](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=9)):

> Le reste semble effectivement bien se dérouler, mis à part la perte de places de stationnement. Notre personnel est très inquiet pour cet hiver. Est-ce que l'arrondissement a prévu des déneigements plus fréquents pour permettre aux citoyens d'avoir accès à des places de stationnement dans les rues? De plus, n'y aurait-il pas moyen de diminuer la piste cyclable à un côté du 15 novembre au 15 avril, par exemple, pour permettre aux automobilistes de se stationner du côté nord de Terrebonne? J'imagine que ce n'est pas possible car la piste cyclable serait trop étroite pour permettre une circulation dans les deux sens...

No reply to that message is in the release.

### The EMSB's letter of November 22, 2024 ([pp. 29–30](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=29))

The letter, on the letterhead of the Office of the Chairman and sent by e-mail to the borough's clerk on November 22, 2024 at 8:32 ([p. 31](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=31)), asks to be tabled as public correspondence at the December council. It refers to « a recent accident involving a school bus and a parent's vehicle that occurred directly in front of St. Monica Elementary School. Thankfully, no one was seriously injured », which it attributes to the narrowing of the roadway; to « the Borough's recent announcement that the bike path will remain operational during the winter months »; to a letter of February 5, 2024 from the chair objecting to the project and to « detailed letters » from the principals of the four schools; and says « Despite assurances in late 2023 that our concerns would be taken into account, the final project proceeded unchanged. » It requests: « The removal of the bike path between Madison Avenue and Cavendish Boulevard for the winter months » and « The creation of a joint committee comprising EMSB specialists and Borough traffic officials to collaboratively design safer conditions for the spring. » The same text was published on the chair's blog (filed in [Terrebonne-open-sources](https://github.com/OpenMobilityData/Terrebonne-open-sources/blob/main/2022-2026-emsb-governing-boards/README.md)); this is the copy as received by the borough, with the signature and the list of copies. On November 25, 2024 the clerk's office sent it to the borough mayor and the five councillors: it « sera déposée à la séance du conseil du 2 décembre 2024 prochain au point correspondance » ([p. 31](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=31)).

The release holds no reply by the borough to the letter, and neither the letter of February 5, 2024 nor the principals' letters that it mentions.

### Willingdon School: the crossing guard and the drop-off zone, November 2024 to February 2025 ([pp. 6–8](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=6), [13–28](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=13))

On November 27, 2024 the principal of Willingdon School asked again for a crossing guard at Terrebonne and Draper: « Since the beginning of this school year, we have had several incidents where cyclist have no[t] stopped at the traffic light or respected a bus drop off (after a day trip) while the students were disembarking »; « Several families at Willingdon have also indicated that cyclists do not stop at the light when crossing. They and their children have nearly been driven into by the users of the cycle path. » ([p. 20](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=20)). The councillor for Loyola answered the next day, spoke with the principal on December 2, 2024 and wrote the same day that the councillor for Notre-Dame-de-Grâce could write a letter in support of the request, to be made to the police (station 9), that the division « is also looking into the parking requests made by a parent and at other possible solutions to ensure that cyclists slow down around the schools », and that « the mayor Ms. Katahwa will be asking to meet with you and the other directors of the EMSB schools to discuss the configuration further » ([p. 7](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=7)). On January 30, 2025 the division asked the principal whether the parking signage needed adjusting; on the header date of February 10, 2025 she answered that the school board's management would take it up in its discussions with the councillor ([p. 13](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=13)).

A parent's letter of November 1, 2024 to the councillor for Notre-Dame-de-Grâce (the writer's identity blacked out) says: « I want to be clear that I fully support the city's efforts to develop and maintain bike paths », and asks that the parking restrictions in front of the school, which it describes as « relics from a previous configuration when the street operated as a two way road », be revised to allow more drop-off space ([pp. 14–15](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=14), [27–28](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=27)). The councillor asked the administration on November 6, 2024 to consider the proposals « séreusement [sic] et revenir avec une réponse » ([p. 27](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=27)); the division wrote to the councillor for Loyola that it usually validates signage changes with the school and saw no difficulty in lengthening the 15-minute drop-off zone ([p. 23](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2026-689/raw/Doc_requis_2026-689.pdf#page=23)).
