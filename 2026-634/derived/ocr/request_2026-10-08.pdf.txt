Madam, Sir,

Under the Act respecting Access to documents held by public bodies and the Protection of personal
information (CQLR, c. A-2.1) (the “Act”), | request access to the documents described below.

This request complements my request of September 11, 2026 concerning the study of rue de Terrebonne
(your file no. 2026-581, acknowledged September 14, 2026). That request covered the final report only
if it had been received by that date (item D3). Since then, Mr. Alexandre Teodoresco, city councillor for
the Loyola district, has indicated that the study was carried out by Les Services EXP inc., has stated at a
public council meeting that the Borough has the study, and has referred at that meeting to figures from
presentation material on the subject. To avoid duplication, this request covers only the items below. Where
a document is already being processed under the earlier request, a single response is fine.

Definitions

“Study” The study of rue de Terrebonne carried out for the Borough by Les Services EXP inc. and
described in my request of September 11, 2026, whatever its formal title.

“Meeting” The regular meeting of the borough council held on October 6, 2026 (recording: https:

“Documents” Documents in any form (section 1 of the Act), including emails with their attachments,
presentations, spreadsheets and datasets, including documents kept on the Borough's
behalf by the consultant or another third party.

A3. Any summary or presentation of the Study’s results prepared by the consultant for the Borough.

Processing

1.     Withheld documents. For any document withheld in whole or in part, I ask that the decision

2.     Severance. Where an exception applies to part of a document, I ask that the remainder be

3.     Material relied on in public. At the Meeting, Mr. Teodoresco stated that the slides contained
