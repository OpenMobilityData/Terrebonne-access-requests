R.S.Q., chapter A-2.1

       An Act respecting Access to documents held by public
         bodies and the Protection of personal information



9. Every person has a right of access, on request, to the documents held by a
public body.

The right does not extend to personal notes written on a document or to
sketches, outlines, drafts, preliminary notes or other documents of the same
nature.

1982, c. 30, s. 9.


39.        A public body may refuse to disclose a study prepared in
connection with a recommendation made within a decision making process
until a decision is made on the recommendation or, if no decision is made,
until five years have elapsed from the date the study was made.

1982, c. 30, s. 39.
