R.S.Q., chapter A-2.1

An Act respecting Access to documents held by public
  bodies and the Protection of personal information



23. No public body may release industrial secrets of a third
person or confidential industrial, financial, commercial,
scientific, technical or union information supplied by a third
person and ordinarily treated by a third person as confidential,
without his consent.

1982, c. 30, s. 23.



24. No public body may release information supplied by a
third person if its disclosure would likely hamper negotiations
in view of a contract, result in losses for the third person or in
considerable profit for another person or substantially reduce
the third person's competitive margin, without his consent.

1982, c. 30, s. 24.
