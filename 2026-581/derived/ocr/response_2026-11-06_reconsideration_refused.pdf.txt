commissioned to recommend, the removal of one of the two bike lanes on rue de Terrebonne each winter, and said that
the elected officials were "now ready to give our orientation to the services" and that this "is what we're going to
propose". Councillor Teodoresco's announcement on his official Facebook page, published afterwards, says that

December 7, 2026. If the borough's position is that no decision has yet been made on the recommendation, | would ask
you to identify the decision still pending and the body that will make it. In any event, the exception will cease to apply
once the council has voted, and | ask that the documents be released at that point at the latest, without a new request.

2. The borough's own practice on this street. On October 30, 2023, the borough published in full the mobility study on rue
de Terrebonne prepared by the same firm (351 pages), on the day it announced the scenario it had chosen and more
than three months before the borough council (February 5, 2024) and city council (February 19, 2024) approved the
project. The press release reads: "Aujourd'hui, l'arrondissement publie les résultats de l'étude et dévoile le scénario
choisi pour le réaménagement de la rue de Terrebonne." The 2026 note stands in the same relation to the measure
announced on October 6, 2026 as the 2023 study stood to the project announced on October 30, 2023. | ask that the
same approach be taken, and, if it is not, that you tell me what distinguishes the two cases: why could the borough
publish the firm's study on this street when it announced its chosen scenario in 2023, but not in 2026?

parts of a document not covered by an exception be released. According to the offer of service released with your
decision (pp. 9-13 of the bundle), the EXP note was to compare winter layout scenarios, including closure during snow-
clearing operations only, and to analyse geometric and operational constraints. The SUM analysis, of which only the

recommendation.
